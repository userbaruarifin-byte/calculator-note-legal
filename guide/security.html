Security & backup

All of these settings are under Security. Open the vault, then tap the shield icon at the top right. The shield only appears when the vault was opened with the right password.

1. Fingerprint / face

Unlocking → Biometric unlock. Once it is on, triple-tap the title and touch the sensor. No need to type the password.

  • A fingerprint always opens the real vault.
  • If you are being forced to open it, ignore the fingerprint, type a different password, and an empty vault opens (see If the password is wrong).
  • Your fingerprint has to be set up in the phone's settings first.

2. While the vault is open

  • Lock when I leave the app (on by default): the vault locks as soon as you switch to another app or press Home. Picking a file, or opening one in another app, does not lock it.
  • Block screenshots (on by default): vault screens cannot be screenshotted or recorded, and show black in the recent apps list.

3. Intruder photo

If someone else tries → Photograph failed attempts (off by default). After 3 wrong passwords in a row, the front camera quietly takes a photo. The photo and a log of the attempts are in the Security log.

  • Camera permission is only asked for when you turn this on.
  • The person entering the wrong password is not told they were photographed.

4. Disguising the app

Disguise → App icon and name. Choose Calculator, Weather, Clock or Compass.

  • The icon and name on your home screen change, including in other apps' Share menus.
  • The app opens as a Weather (type a city), Clock (with a stopwatch) or Compass that really works.
  • Getting into the vault: triple-tap the title at the top of the Weather / Clock / Compass screen quickly, just as on the calculator.
Icon gone from the home screen?Some launchers drop the old icon when the disguise changes. Find the app in the app drawer and add it to the home screen again. Its name in Settings → Apps stays "Calculator".

5. Panic exit

Under pressure → Panic exit (off by default). While the vault is open:

  • put the phone face down on a table, or
  • shake the phone hard a few times,

and the vault locks at once and goes back to the calculator.

Be awareHolding the phone above your face while lying down can read as "face down". Try the feature out before you rely on it.

6. Backup & moving to a new phone

The vault exists only on this phone. It is not included in Google's automatic backup. If the phone is lost, broken or replaced without a backup, the contents are gone with it.

Making a backup

  1. Backup → Back up to a file.
  2. Create a Backup password, then type it again under Type it again. It can differ from the vault password. Do not forget it.
  3. Choose where to save the backup file, for example Google Drive or Downloads, then tap Back up.

The backup file holds your notes (with their pictures), hidden files, Shared Content and links, your own formulas, the Cash Log and reminders, all encrypted with the backup password.

Restoring on a new phone

  1. Install the app and create a vault password.
  2. Open Security → Backup → Restore from a backup.
  3. Pick the backup file and enter the backup password.
Safe to repeatA restore only adds what is in the backup and never overwrites what is already there. Restoring the same file twice does not duplicate anything.

7. Changing the password

Open Encrypted Note, open the side menu, then choose Change password. You will need the old password. The option only appears when the vault was opened with the right password.
