Secret vault

Behind the calculator is a locked vault for notes, photos, videos, files, links and reminders. Everything in it is encrypted and stays on your phone.

1. Opening the vault

  1. On the calculator, tap the word Calculator at the top three times quickly (within a second). There is no special button. That is on purpose, so nobody else knows the vault is there.
  2. The first time: create a password (at least 4 characters), then type it again to confirm.
  3. After that: triple-tap, enter the password, and the vault opens.
  4. To close it, tap the padlock icon at the top right. The vault also locks itself when you leave the app.
The password cannot be recoveredThere is no "forgot password". If the password is lost, nobody can open what is in the vault. Write it down, and make a backup.
Faster with a fingerprintOnce inside, open the shield icon → Biometric unlock. See Security.

2. If the password is wrong

A wrong password is not rejected. It opens a different, empty vault instead. This is a safety feature: if someone forces you to open the app, type another password and all they see is an empty vault.

  • Your real contents are not lost. If the vault is suddenly empty, you most likely mistyped. Lock it and enter the right password.
  • Anything saved while a wrong password is in use goes into that password's own empty vault, separate from your real one.
  • A fingerprint always opens the real vault.

3. What is inside

Once it is open, there are four cards:

CardWhat it holds
Encrypted NotePrivate notes, checklists and pictures.
Hidden FilePhotos, videos and files you pick yourself to hide.
Shared ContentAnything you send from another app with its Share button, links included. Link playlists live here too.
RemindersSecret reminders.

The shield icon at the top right opens the Security settings.

4. Encrypted Note

  • Tap Create a note for an ordinary note, or Start a checklist for a tick list.
  • Inside a note, the Checklist button at the top turns the selected lines into a checklist. The note's card shows progress, for example "2 of 5 done".
  • You can add pictures and give a note a category.

5. Hidden File: hiding photos & files

  1. Open Hidden File, then tap Hide files.
  2. Pick photos, videos or files.
  3. The app encrypts a copy, checks that the copy is identical to the original, then deletes the original from the gallery or folder. Android may ask you to confirm the deletion. Tap Allow.
Files are moved, not copiedOnce hidden, a photo is no longer in the gallery. To bring it back, open it in the vault → three dots → Unhide (save a normal copy).
  • If the original could not be deleted (for example you declined Android's confirmation), the file is marked Original still on this device. Tap Try removing the original again to retry.
  • Photos, videos and audio open right inside the vault. Swipe sideways for the next one. Videos have play, 10-second skip, speed and full screen.

6. Shared Content: sending from other apps

  1. In another app (Gallery, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, a browser…), tap its Share button.
  2. Choose Calculator. If the app is disguised, choose its disguise name, for example Weather.
  3. What you shared (a photo, video, file, link or text) is saved, encrypted, in Shared Content. A file's original is moved, as in Hidden File.

Inside Shared Content, the Add button also offers Import a file and Paste a link.

7. Link playlists

Group your saved links by purpose, for example Study, Work, Recipes or Watch later. Any name you like.

  1. Open Shared Content, then tap Playlists at the top right.
  2. Tap New playlist, name it, then tick the links to put in it (or paste a new one).
  3. Tap a playlist chip at the top to see what is in it. Its links are numbered in the order they were saved.
  4. A link that is not in a playlist yet has a +≡ button to add it straight away.
  5. When you add a link with Add link, you can pick its playlist there and then.
  • Long-press a playlist chip to add links, rename it, or remove the playlist. Removing a playlist does not delete its links.
  • A link can be in one playlist only.

8. Albums

Photos and files in Hidden File or Shared Content can be grouped too: open an item's three dots → New album… or pick an existing album. The album choices appear at the top once something has been filed.

9. Reminders: secret reminders

  1. Open Reminders, then tap New reminder.
  2. Fill in Remind me to (the secret part), Note (optional), and the date and time.
  3. Fill in The notification says with a cover text for the notification, for example "Drink water". The real reminder never appears in a notification.
  4. Choose a Sound: Alarm (rings even when the phone is on silent), Normal or Silent. Then tap Save.
It may be a few minutes lateAndroid sometimes holds notifications back to save battery, so a reminder can arrive a few minutes after its time.
