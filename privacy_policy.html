Privacy Policy — Calculator Note

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Calculator Note ("the app", "we", "us") is a calculator app with an optional hidden, password-protected area for notes and files. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and your choices — for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux, and Web versions of the app.

1. Your notes and files stay on your device

Everything you keep in the protected area — notes, and any files you hide there — is stored only on your device, never on our servers. Specifically:

  • Notes are stored locally in an encrypted database, encrypted with a device-specific key plus an additional layer of app-level encryption.
  • Files you hide (photos, videos, documents, audio — anything you pick or share into the app) are encrypted with a key generated on your device and kept in your device's secure OS storage, then stored in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded. Links you save are stored the same way.
  • Your unlock password is stored in your device's secure OS storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain), salted and encrypted. We never see it, and it is never transmitted anywhere. There is no recovery mechanism: we cannot read it, reset it, or recover the notes behind it.
  • We have no server, database, or backend that stores your notes, files, or password — if you delete the app or lose your device without a backup, that data cannot be recovered by us because we never had it.

2. Hiding a file moves it

When you hide a file, the app encrypts a copy into the protected area, checks that the encrypted copy reads back identically to the original, and only then deletes the original from where it was — your gallery, your Downloads folder, or wherever you picked it from. That deletion is what makes the file disappear from those places.

  • Nothing is ever deleted unless the encrypted copy was written and verified first.
  • Android may show its own confirmation before deleting. That prompt comes from the operating system, not from us, and you have to accept it.
  • If a file cannot be deleted — the source is read-only, the system refuses, or another app shared only a copy with us — the app says so for that specific file and the original stays where it is. We do not claim a file was removed when it wasn't.
  • Deletion only ever applies to the file you just hid. The app does not search your device for other copies of it, and does not touch anything you hid previously.

4. Device permissions and security features

The app asks your operating system for a permission only when a feature you are using needs one. None of these send anything off your device.

  • Camera — used for one thing only: the optional photo of a failed unlock attempt described above. This feature ships turned off; if you never turn it on, the app never asks for camera access and never opens the camera. The app does not record video or audio.
  • Fingerprint / face unlock — if you enable it, your device performs the check and tells the app only whether it succeeded. Your fingerprint and face data stay with your operating system's secure hardware; the app never receives, stores, or transmits them.
  • Files and photos you choose — the app can only read the specific files you pick in the system file picker, or that another app shares with it. It does not browse your storage or scan your photo library.
  • Screenshot blocking — while your protected area is open, the app asks Android to block screenshots and screen recording and to hide the preview shown in the app switcher. This is a local display setting; it collects nothing. You can turn it off in the app's security settings.

5. What happens when a password doesn't match

There is no second password to set up, and nothing extra is stored to make this work.

A password that isn't yours is never rejected. Instead of an error, it opens a protected area of its own, which starts out empty — no notes, no hidden files, no shared content. Anything saved while such a password is open is kept separately from your real notes and files, stays on your device only, and is covered by the same encryption described above.

The area a given password opens is derived from that password together with a random key created on your device when the app is first used. Two consequences follow: the same password always returns to the same area, and two different passwords never see each other's contents. The password itself is not stored, and it does not appear in the name of anything saved on your device.

We cannot tell you, anyone else, or a court which password is which, or how many of these areas exist on your device — none of that information ever leaves your device, and we never had it. Unlocking with a fingerprint or face always opens your real area, never one of these.

Failed attempts are still recorded. Each one is counted in the app's security log, and if you turned the optional photo feature on, the third wrong password in a row is still photographed as described above — the app simply does not say on screen that anything was wrong.

What this does not protect against. These areas are kept apart by how they are named on your device, not by separate encryption keys. Someone with full technical access to your unlocked device — for example, using developer tools — could tell that more than one such area exists. This feature is meant for the situation where someone asks you to open the app in front of them; it is not designed to withstand forensic examination.

6. Desktop and Web versions

The Windows, macOS, Linux, and Web versions of the app show no ads and use no advertising SDK. They use the same anonymous device ID and RevenueCat-based purchase check described above for premium unlock via web checkout (Stripe, processed by RevenueCat) — we never receive your payment card details.

7. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: in the EEA/UK, you'll see a consent prompt (via Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time you open the app, where you can decline personalized ads. On iOS, you can also decline via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Restore purchases: available anytime from the paywall screen, using the anonymous device ID above.
  • Security features are yours to turn off: biometric unlock, locking when you leave the app, screenshot blocking and the photo of failed attempts are all in the app's security settings, and each can be switched off at any time. The behaviour described in section 5 is not a setting — it is simply how the app responds to a password that doesn't match — so there is nothing to turn on, and nothing extra stored about it.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored notes, hidden files, security-log photos and your password, since nothing is stored remotely. You may also delete individual notes, files and log entries at any time within the app. A file you hid can be saved back out to normal storage first, using "Unhide".

8. Children's privacy

Calculator Note is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
