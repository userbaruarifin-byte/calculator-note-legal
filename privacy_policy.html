Privacy Policy — Calculator Note

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Calculator Note ("the app", "we", "us") is a calculator app with an optional hidden, password-protected notes feature. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and your choices — for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux, and Web versions of the app.

1. Your notes stay on your device

Everything you write in the notes feature is stored only on your device, never on our servers. Specifically:

  • Notes are stored locally in an encrypted database, encrypted with a device-specific key plus an additional layer of app-level encryption.
  • Your unlock password is stored in your device's secure OS keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain), salted and hashed. We never see it, and it is never transmitted anywhere.
  • We have no server, database, or backend that stores your notes or password — if you delete the app or lose your device without a backup, that data cannot be recovered by us because we never had it.

We do not use Firebase, Crashlytics, or any general-purpose analytics SDK. We do not track you across other apps or websites, beyond the standard advertising identifiers used by Google AdMob for ad delivery.

3. Desktop and Web versions

The Windows, macOS, Linux, and Web versions of the app show no ads and use no advertising SDK. They use the same anonymous device ID and RevenueCat-based purchase check described above for premium unlock via web checkout (Stripe, processed by RevenueCat) — we never receive your payment card details.

4. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: in the EEA/UK, you'll see a consent prompt (via Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time you open the app, where you can decline personalized ads. On iOS, you can also decline via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Restore purchases: available anytime from the paywall screen, using the anonymous device ID above.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored notes and your password, since nothing is stored remotely. You may also delete individual notes at any time within the app.

5. Children's privacy

Calculator Note is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
